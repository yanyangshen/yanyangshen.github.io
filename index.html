Welcome

Hi there, I'm Yanyang.

I am a master's student in Educational Economy and Management at East China Normal University, where my work focuses on education policy, educational inequality, and postsecondary pathways.

My research examines how education policies and institutional arrangements shape students' opportunities and longer-term outcomes. I am particularly interested in economics of education, higher education policy evaluation, nontraditional students, and economic mobility.

Before my master's studies, I earned a bachelor's degree in International Economics and Trade at Hohai University. I also studied at the University of Tsukuba in Japan as an exchange student.

What I have worked on

Research Experience

Adult Higher Education and Learner Outcomes

2025–2026

Led a first-author study using survey data from 11,408 adult higher education graduates, examining vocational tracking and subsequent wage mobility through ordered logistic regression, matching, weighting, and sensitivity analyses. Also analyzed 9,880 adult learners using latent class analysis and regression in a study later accepted by Open Education Research.

Higher Education Policy Evaluation

2024–Present

Led a difference-in-differences study with panel data from 285 Chinese cities (2007–2022), evaluating China's Double First-Class university initiative and urban innovation, entrepreneurship, and economic development. Related work is part of a Ministry of Education major research project (24JZD015).

Education Finance and Policy

2026–Present

Contributed to a Shanghai Municipal Education Commission commissioned study on extending compulsory education in China, reviewing education expenditure, student aid, and central–local fiscal transfers using 2015–2024 policy and fiscal data.
