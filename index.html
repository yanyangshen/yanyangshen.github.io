Hi there! I'm Yanyang (艳阳).

My name means “bright sunshine” in Chinese. My parents chose it hoping I'd grow up cheerful and outgoing. I do enjoy my quiet time (probably more than they imagined), but I also love a long, honest conversation with close friends. And when someone's having a hard day, I'm always happy to lend an ear. So maybe the sunshine part worked out after all.

I'm currently a master's student in the Department of Educational Administration at East China Normal University's Faculty of Education, studying Educational Economy and Management. I use quantitative methods to explore questions about education policy and equity. I'm especially interested in disadvantaged students and nontraditional learners, and in how policies and institutions shape their educational opportunities and longer-term outcomes.

Before that, I earned my bachelor's degree in economics at Hohai University, where I developed an economist's way of looking at things and strengthened my math skills. During my master's, I spent a semester studying abroad at the University of Tsukuba in Japan. It gave me fresh perspectives on the world—and, even better, a wonderful group of friends from different countries. All these experiences have helped make me who I am today.

Curious about the person behind the research? Have a look at Bits & Pieces, my little collection of things I love and moments I want to remember.
