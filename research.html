RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Projects

Adult Higher Education and Learner Outcomes

2025–2026

Led a first-author study with 11,408 adult higher education graduates, examining secondary vocational tracking and subsequent wage mobility using ordered logit, matching, weighting, and sensitivity analyses. Also analyzed 9,880 adult learners through latent class analysis and regression in a collaborative study accepted by Open Education Research.

Higher Education Policy Evaluation

2024–Present

Designed and led a difference-in-differences study with panel data from 285 Chinese cities (2007–2022), evaluating China's Double First-Class university initiative and urban innovation, entrepreneurship, and economic development. Part of a Ministry of Education major research project (24JZD015).

Education Finance and Policy Research

2026–Present

Independently drafted an education-finance section of a commissioned policy study on extending compulsory education in China, analyzing educational expenditure, central–local fiscal transfers, and student-aid data from 2015–2024.

Other projects and collaborations
  • Master's thesis: University program restructuring and emerging industry development in China (in progress).
  • Shanghai and OECD education expenditure (2024): comparative analysis of doctoral attainment, funding, STEM fields, and program expansion.
  • Vocational bachelor's policy (2025): a National Social Science Fund key project on policy design and institutional transition.
  • Shanghai higher education clusters (2025–2026): a comparative case study of Greater Boston and regional innovation systems.
  • K–12 curriculum leadership (2024–Present): research design and conceptual framework development.
