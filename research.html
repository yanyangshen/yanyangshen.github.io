RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Projects

Research on Adult Higher Education and Learner Outcomes

2025–2026
More ↓Less ↑
  • Designed and led a first-author study of 11,408 adult higher education graduates in China, using ordered logistic regression, matching, weighting, and sensitivity analyses to examine disparities in wage mobility associated with secondary vocational tracking and their heterogeneity across employment contexts.
  • Contributed to a study of 9,880 adult learners at an open university using latent class analysis and regression to examine motivational profiles, learning processes, and competence development; independently drafted the full initial manuscript and participated in subsequent revisions, resulting in a second-author publication accepted by Open Education Research (CSSCI-indexed).

PI: Prof. Yaozong Hu, East China Normal University

More ↓Less ↑
  • Conceived and led a first-author difference-in-differences study using panel data from 285 Chinese cities (2007–2022) to evaluate the effects of China's Double First-Class university initiative on entrepreneurship, innovation, university–industry collaboration, and urban economic development.
